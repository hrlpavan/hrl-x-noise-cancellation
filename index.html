Developer Story // Acoustic Physics

How Destructive Interference Creates Absolute Silence

Grounding active noise cancellation in the Branch Education blueprint: sound is not an electronic signal, but physical longitudinal compressions and rarefactions propagating through air molecules at 343 meters per second.

01. COMPRESSIONS & RAREFACTIONS

Physical Pressure Annihilation

Room fans push air into oscillating high-pressure (+P) and low-pressure (-P) packets. When the boAt 40mm transducer produces an exact equal-and-opposite rarefaction, air molecules return to equilibrium atmospheric pressure P₀ = 101,325 Pa. Net eardrum movement drops to zero.

02. SPATIAL COHERENCE CEILING

The 1,200 Hz Active Hand-off

Because sound travels at 343 m/s, wavelength scales inversely with frequency (λ = c/f). At 120 Hz, λ = 2.86 m, ensuring perfect phase coherence. Above 1,200 Hz, wavelength shrinks to 28 cm, where active DSP safely hands off to passive acoustic foam absorption to prevent constructive hiss.

03. SUPERPOSITION INVARIANCE

Preserving Music & Speech

Acoustic waves in air obey linear differential equations. The speaker plays [Music - Noise], while external air injects [+Noise]. At the eardrum: [Music - Noise] + Noise = Music. Your favourite audio is delivered with 100% fidelity while room noise is destroyed.
